## Instagram / Facebook caption

Fuel in Karnataka, office in Kerala?! ⛽ Welcome to Talapady 😄
Talapady = Tala/Taar (small stream) + Paadi (hamlet on its bank): the little village by the stream 🌊

Until 1956, Kasaragod, Manjeshwar and Talapady were all one district: South Canara, one Tulunadu. On 1 November 1956, states were redrawn by language. Kasaragod went to Kerala, Talapady stayed with Karnataka. And according to a news report, in 2020, officials checking 1925 survey maps found the border running right through a petrol pump!

The stream came first. The border came later.
