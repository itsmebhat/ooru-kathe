Bijai today = malls, hospitals, KSRTC 🛍️🚌

Bijai 1000 years ago = a seed store? 🌾

"Beeja" means seed. A popular belief says Bijai was once where the village kept its seeds safe. In a farming village, lose your seeds and you lose next year's food!

Today we go to Bijai to spend everything… back then, they went there to save everything 😄
