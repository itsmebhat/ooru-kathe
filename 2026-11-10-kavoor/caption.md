KAVOOR 🌳 Its name means "protected"… but for years, it couldn't protect its own lake 

📜 Story 1, the legend: around 700 years ago, Sage Kavera did penance here and installed a Shiva linga. Kavera → Kavoor?

🌿 Story 2, the researcher: KAVU = a sacred grove, those thick patches of trees we never cut, where daivas and nagas live. Kavoor = the village of protected groves.

🛕 The Mahalingeshwara temple was the protector of NINE villages, the Kuloor Magane: Kavoor, Kuloor, Kunjathbail and even Uliya, the old island village! Its wooden chariot was gifted by the Keladi kings in the 1600s.
😢 The twist? Kavoor lake (8+ acres, one of the city's biggest) slowly turned into a sewage dump. It took Rs 8 crore and a Smart City cleanup in 2023 to save it.
