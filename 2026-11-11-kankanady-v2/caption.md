## Instagram / Facebook caption

"Send him to Kankanady!" 😂 We've all said it. But do you know who actually built that hospital? 🤔

🇩🇪 A German maths teacher, Fr Augustus Muller (1841–1910), came to teach at St Aloysius College. Seeing poor people who couldn't afford a doctor, he started giving free homeopathy medicine under a banyan tree 🌳

🏥 1880: that tree became a small dispensary, and then Father Muller's hospital.

🙏 From 1883, he cared for leprosy patients that even their own families had thrown out. In 1892 his leprosy home moved to Kankanady, and it's still running today.

🏅 The British gave him the Kaisar-i-Hind medal, but he never went back. He died in Kankanady in 1910, with his patients.
