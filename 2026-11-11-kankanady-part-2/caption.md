PART 2 🧠 Why Kankanady really mattered

150 years ago, if someone in Kudla had a mental illness, the family had 3 options: the home, the temple… or the chain 😶 There was no word for "mental health", only madness, possession, bad spirits.
🗺️ The twist: Mangaluru wasn't even in Karnataka back then! We were part of the Madras Presidency, while Bangalore was in the Kingdom of Mysore. The nearest asylum (Bangalore, 1847, later NIMHANS) was in another state, and Madras was 700+ km away 😢
🏥 Then in 1965, Dr K. Sudhakar Shetty, one of India's first trained psychiatrists, started a psychiatry department at Father Muller's, right here in Kankanady. He served there for 52 years 🙏
For years we joked "send him to Kankanady". But for so many families, it was the first place that said: we'll take care of him ❤️
Thank you, Kankanady. Special thanks to @arundsouza for sharing this history.
