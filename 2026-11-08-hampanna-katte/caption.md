Hampanna Katte is named after ONE man?! 🤯 | Mangaluru | Ooru Kathe

Over 100 years ago, a man named Appanna sat at his well at this very junction and gave every tired traveller water and a piece of jaggery. For free. 🙏💧
People called the spot Appanna Katte. Over the years, it became Hampanna Katte. ❤️
And the well? Covered up, forgotten, and dug up again during road work in 2020. Still full of water.
