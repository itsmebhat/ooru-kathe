## Instagram / Facebook caption

Bondel… sounds very Tulu, right? Well… it might be FRENCH! 🇫🇷🤯

📜 Story 1: In 1873, a French missionary priest saw the green valley and called it "bon vale", good valley. Over time, it became BONDEL. (That's from the church's own history!)

🏡 Story 2: In Tulu and Konkani, "del" means a small hamlet. That's why we have Niddel, Biddel… and Bondel.

⛪ 1913: a tiny prayer hall. 1923: Bondel gets its own church. 100 years later: a pilgrimage spot where thousands gather every August 10th.

🚗 And today? Airport Road, glass towers, honking traffic… and poet Sarvajna at the junction, wondering where the valley went. 😄
