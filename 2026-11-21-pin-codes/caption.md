Your PIN code says how NEW your area is 📮

5 = South India
56 = Karnataka (560001 = Bengaluru)
575 = Mangaluru city · 574 = villages around · 576 = Udupi
And the last 3 digits?
001 = Hampankatta, Car Street, Bolar: the old heart
002 = Kadri, Kankanady, Balmatta, Jeppu
003 = Kodialbail, Kudroli, Boloor
010 = Panambur · 014 = Surathkal
017 = Ullal · 023 = Talapady · 030 = Katipalla

Small number, old Kudla. Big number, new Kudla 😄
PIN codes started on 15 August 1972, thanks to Shriram Bhikaji Velankar.
