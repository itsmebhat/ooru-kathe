## Instagram / Facebook caption

Mangaluru = named after a CROCODILE? 🐊

Everyone says Mangaladevi temple, case closed! But the late Tulu researcher Dr. Ravindra Mundkur opened the case again…

⛵ Around 545 AD, a Greek traveller, Cosmas "the voyager of India", wrote down our town's name as MANGARUTH.

🐊 Mundkur said the old name was MANGAR, an ancient Munda word meaning CROCODILE! In Tulu today we say "mudale", which shows how old the word is.

🌊 Back then, the Netravati was full of crocodiles. And the old ferry point? Boatmen called it "Mangar kariya", the crocodile ferry!
