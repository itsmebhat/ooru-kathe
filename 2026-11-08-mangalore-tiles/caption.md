Description:
That red roof on every old Kudla house? It has a story. ❤️
In 1865, German missionary Georg Plebst started making tiles on the banks of the Nethravathi, with just two workers and a few bullocks. The clay came from our own rivers and was full of iron, which is why the tiles are red. 🧱
Soon, Mangalore tiles were shipped to Burma, Sri Lanka, Africa, the Middle East and even Australia. Mumbai's famous CST station (Victoria Terminus) has them on its roof!
By the 1990s, Kudla had around 75 tile factories. Today, only a handful are still running.
