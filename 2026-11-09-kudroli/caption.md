## Instagram / Facebook caption

Kudroli 🏝️ or Kudroli 🐎? What does the name really mean?

In Tulu, KUDURU = island 🏝️ and OLI = hamlet. Put them together and you get "a village on an island"!

Tulu researcher Ravindra Mundkur says a river once flowed around this place. The river moved away, but the name stayed, like a fossil 🦴
Then there's the popular story: KUDRE = horse 🐎, where Tipu Sultan's army is said to have kept its horses. Kudre-halli → Kudroli.

🛕 In 1912, Sree Narayana Guru consecrated the Gokarnanatha temple here. Today it's the heart of Mangaluru Dasara ✨
