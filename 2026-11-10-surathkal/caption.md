## Instagram / Facebook caption

SURATHKAL 🌊 Half of India thinks it's from Surat… Sorry Surat, nothing to do with you! 😄

📜 Story 1, the legend: SHIRA = head, KAL = stone → "Shiradakal", the head stone. The demon Kharasura was carrying a Shiva linga on his head, and where it fell, on the rocks by the sea, stands the Sadashiva temple 🛕 Some even say a piece of Ravana's Atma Linga landed here!
🔍 Story 2, the researcher: the late Tulu researcher Dr. Ravindra Mundkur said the original name was TORATT-KALL: rocks washed by the waves 🪨🌊

✨ The beautiful part? Go to Surathkal beach today. The waves still crash on those rocks, right below the temple. Both stories point to the same place!
