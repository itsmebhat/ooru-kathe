## Instagram / Facebook caption

Ever wondered why it's called LADYHILL? 🤔 The answer is… schoolgirls! 👧📚

🇫🇷 1885: a nun from France, Mother Marie des Anges, arrives in Mangaluru. The locals ask for just one thing: "Please start a school for our girls."

👑 1887: it's Queen Victoria's Golden Jubilee, so it becomes Victoria Girls' School.

⛰️ 1921: the school moves to a hill in Urwa.
❤️ Out of respect for the girls studying there, people started calling it… Ladyhill.

🤯 The twist? As per Corporation records, the circle was never officially named Ladyhill. Yet for 100+ years, that's what everyone calls it!
