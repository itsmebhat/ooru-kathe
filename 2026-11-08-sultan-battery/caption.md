## Instagram / Facebook caption

Tipu Sultan built THIS to stop warships. ⚔️🌊

In 1784, Tipu built a black stone watchtower at Boloor, right by the Gurupura river. Enemy ships could sail in from the Arabian Sea, so this tower held cannons, worked as a lookout and served as a small naval station. 🏰

💡 "Battery" isn't a phone battery. It means a group of cannons!
🕳️ A secret tunnel to Mysore? Historians aren't sure. But the gunpowder room underneath is real.

240+ years, still watching the river. 👀
