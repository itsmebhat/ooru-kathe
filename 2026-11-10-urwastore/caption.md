## Instagram / Facebook caption

URWA STORE 🚌 It's a bus stop. It's an area. It's even the official name of the locality. But… which store? 🤔

🏘️ First, URWA: the late Tulu researcher Dr. Ravindra Mundkur said it's URU + VA. Uru/Ooru = a village or settlement, va = a place where people live. Yes, the same OORU as in Ooru Kathe! 😄

🏪 In the 1970s, M. Subraya Pai built a building on the road to Ashoknagar. Downstairs: a small grocery shop called Urwa Store. Upstairs: an ESI hospital and typewriting classes! The shop even ran a chit fund, so locals called it "Urwa Fundangadi" 💰

🚏 People waiting for buses gathered in front of the shop, so conductors started shouting "Urwa Store! Urwa Store!" And the name stuck.

🏗️ The twist? In April 2025, the building was demolished for road widening. The store is gone, but every bus conductor still shouts its name 📢
