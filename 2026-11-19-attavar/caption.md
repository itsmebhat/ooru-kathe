Attavar people, I have some news 😄

You've been living on the other side your whole life… and nobody told you!

Atta = the other bank of a river

Var / ar = an open field

So Attavar = the open land on the other side of the river 🌊
And that little "R" is everywhere in Kudla: Bolar, Kottara, even Mangar, the oldest name of Mangaluru!
Today, thousands arrive at Mangaluru Central station every day, and the first place they reach is literally called "the other side" 🚆
