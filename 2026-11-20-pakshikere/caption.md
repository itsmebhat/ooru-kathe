## Instagram / Facebook caption

Pakshikere = Pakshi (bird) + Kere (lake) 🐦

But go there in summer… where's the lake? 🤔

According to a Times of India report, government records show Pakshikere once had SIX lakes. These were keres, rainwater tanks full of birds and even peacocks. Elders remember Udupi Mutt elephants stopping here to drink on their way to Kateel 🐘

One by one, the lakes disappeared. But now locals are doing the impossible: the old Katipalla pond, once a dumping ground, has clean water again 💧

And in 1960, on a hillock donated by Mrs. Elise (Elizabeth) Miranda, a small chapel opened for St. Jude, the saint of impossible causes. Today it's one of the six official pilgrim shrines of the Mangalore Diocese.
