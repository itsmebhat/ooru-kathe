## Instagram / Facebook caption

There's a village near Udupi named after a bed. A bed made of stone 🪨

Manchakal = mancha (cot) + kallu (rock). A Tulu researcher thinks wandering Jain and Buddhist monks rested on rock beds like these, maybe 2,000 years ago. Nobody has checked yet.

And the same rocky land gave us Udupi Mallige 🤍: from three men walking 100 km to Bhatkal in 1934, to freedom fighter K. Lakshminarayana Sharma bringing jasmine to Bantakal in the 1950s.
Two communities. One flower.
