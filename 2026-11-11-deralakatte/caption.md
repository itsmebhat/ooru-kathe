DERALAKATTE 🎓 University Town… but what does the name actually MEAN? 🤔

We searched everywhere: history blogs, Wikipedia, Kannada sites, even a Tulu researcher's list of Mangaluru place names. NOTHING 😅

🕵️ Clue 1: KATTE = a raised platform under a big shady tree, where tired travellers rested (like Hampankatte!)

🕵️ Clue 2: DERALA = ??? One theory online says "a resting spot of the Dera people"… but we couldn't find a single book or record that says so!
😂 The twist? A place built for resting is now full of students who never get to rest. Exams, practicals, night shifts, chai at 2 am!

👇 Ask your grandparents, your landlord, the oldest uncle at the junction: what does DERALA mean? If we crack it, Part 2 has YOUR name on it!
