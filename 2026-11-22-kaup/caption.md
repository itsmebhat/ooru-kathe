## Instagram / Facebook caption

Kaup isn't named after the lighthouse… it may be named after a GODDESS 🙏👀

🔍 Mystery 1: the name
In Tulu, kāpuni means to watch, to guard. The British just spelled it Kaup.
And this rock really did guard the sea:
🏰 1743: Keladi sea fort
💣 1790s: Tipu's cannon battery
🗼 1901: British lighthouse
3 rulers, 1 rock, same job 😄 A little stream even splits the beach between Tipu's rock and the lighthouse!

🔍 Mystery 2: 3 Mari temples
Tradition traces them to Dandina Mari, the army's goddess, brought by the Keladi soldiers. And this soldier town was also called Hoovina Hitthilu, the garden of flowers 🌼

Maybe the name was never about the fort… but about her: Kapuda Appe, the mother who guards Kapu 🙏
