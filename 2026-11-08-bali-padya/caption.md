## Instagram / Facebook caption

Every Deepavali, Tulunadu invites a king back home. But the invitation is IMPOSSIBLE to accept. 🪔👑
On Bali Padya, Tulu families walk into the paddy fields at dusk, light the thudar, and call out to King Bali: "Come back to the land you ruled!" KOOOO! 🔥🌾

But only when…
🐂 a stone bull bellows
🍓 fruit grows on a black stone
👰 an old woman becomes a bride
⛵ and he comes on a boat with a hole in it!

So he can never really come back. But every year, we call him anyway. Because it's not an invitation. It's a thank you for the harvest, the cattle and the land. 🙏
