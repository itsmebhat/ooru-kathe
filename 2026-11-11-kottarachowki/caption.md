KOTTARA CHOWKI 🚦 Thousands of us get stuck there every day… but what IS a Kottara? 🤔

🛂 CHOWKI = an old checkpost where people and goods were stopped and checked. Honestly, not much has changed 😄

📜 A Tulu researcher gave two meanings:
1️⃣ KOTA + ARA = the open field of the Kota people
2️⃣ A storage area, basically Mangaluru's ancient godown 📦
🤔 Wait, Kota? Like Kota near Kundapura, home of Shivarama Karanth? Any connection? We honestly don't know!
😂 The twist? Long ago, Kottara was where things got stored safely. Today, it's where YOU get stored… in traffic!
