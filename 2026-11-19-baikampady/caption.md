Baikampady 1000 years ago = Buddhist monks? 🧡

A Tulu researcher, the late Dr. Ravindra Mundkur, believed Baikampady may mean "the settlement of wandering Buddhist monks". He also linked an old name, Maikala, to a temple of Mayadevi, the Buddha's mother. And Kadri's famous bronze idol from 968 AD is called Lokeshwara, a Buddhist name.
