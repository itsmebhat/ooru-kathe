## Instagram / Facebook caption

Every year, this temple's first prasada goes to a Muslim family 🙏 Here's why.

Mulki was once an island: Volalanke, "an island inside a river" 🏝️
Where does the name come from?

1️⃣ Moolike: medicinal herbs (the temple legend says Hanuman mistook it for Lanka and herbs fell here 😄)
2️⃣ Mulk: a word traders used for land
3️⃣ Kanakadasa named it in the 16th century, as tradition says

Jain Savanta kings ruled here for centuries, and the Portuguese once demanded 800 loads of rice a year from Karnad next door!

And Bappanadu? Tradition says a Muslim trader, Bappa Beary, built the temple with the Jain king's help. The first prasada still goes to his family ❤️

Today the island is one of India's top surfing spots 🏄
