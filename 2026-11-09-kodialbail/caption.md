## Instagram / Facebook caption

🏝️ Kodialbail = Kudla's paddy fields 🌾

KODIAL = what Konkani speakers call Mangaluru. BAIL = a low, wet paddy field in Tulu.

But here's the twist 🤯 Tulu researcher Ravindra Mundkur says the Phalguni (our Gurupura river) once flowed right through Kodialbail. It cut across today's M G Road near T A Pai hall and met the sea near Kudroli. His proof? Water-worn river pebbles still buried in the soil 🪨
The river moved away, and the fields became roads. But two names still remember it:

🏝️ Kudroli, the island
🌾 Kodialbail, the fields
🚗 Next time you're stuck in traffic at Kodialbail, remember you're standing in an old river valley!
