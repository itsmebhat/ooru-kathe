## Instagram / Facebook caption

"Send him to Kankanady!" 😄 Every Mangalorean has heard this one…
But what if Kankanady deserves to be famous for something much BIGGER? 👑

The late Tulu researcher Dr. Ravindra Mundkur broke the name into two:
🏹 KANKA = an ancient tribe, mentioned even in the Mahabharata
🏘️ NAADI = naadu, a group of villages

The twist? 🤯 He says over centuries Kanka became GANGA, as in the Western Gangas, the kings of Talakadu, who ruled southern Karnataka for 600+ years. Historians still don't know where they came from before 350 AD. Mundkur's theory: maybe right here, in Kankanady!

🌊 Back then, the Netravati flowed past Kankanady and met the sea at Pandeshwar.

🏴‍☠️ And Karambar, near our airport? Possibly home to sea pirates who became the Kadamba kings!

It's a theory, not proven. But next time someone says "send him to Kankanady", tell them it's not a crazy place. It could be the birthplace of a dynasty 😎
