## Instagram / Facebook caption

Every year, a church near Kinnigoli honours three Hindu families 🙏 The reason goes back to Tipu Sultan.

In 1784, Tipu's soldiers destroyed the new church at Kirem and took Christians captive. Tradition says three Guttu Bunt families stood by their Christian neighbours, and they're still honoured at the Kirem church feast, more than 240 years later ❤️

And right there is a place with a curious name: Guttakadu.

Theory 1: Guttu + kaadu = the guttu's forest (power) 🏡

Theory 2: Gutta + kaadu = a forest hill (nature) ⛰️
